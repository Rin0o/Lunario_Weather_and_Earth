# Vignettes

Engravings shown beside screen titles and in empty states, `<name>.png`. `npm run images` prints them in the same
brown ink as the botanical plates (see [../plates/SOURCES.md](../plates/SOURCES.md)).

| Vignette  | Screen               | Engraving                                         | Source                                                                                           |
| --------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `lua`     | Céu                  | _Full Moon_, Claude Mellan, 1635                  | [CC0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Full_Moon_MET_DP822421.jpg), The Met (DP822421)    |
| `regador` | A minha horta, empty | _Watering can_ (Arrosoir), Félix Leblanc, c. 1850 | [CC0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Watering_can_MET_DP875321.jpg), The Met (DP875321) |

The Moon was cut out of the hatched sky along its edge, the outside whitened, and its tones
stretched so the paper of the print reads as white. The watering can was trimmed to the
drawing, leaving out the handwritten caption below it.
